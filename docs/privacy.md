# MusicDecoded Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

MusicDecoded turns songs into chords. This policy explains what the app and its server collect, why, and
what you can do about it. Contact: [jordan879upbeat@gmail.com](mailto:jordan879upbeat@gmail.com).

## What stays on your phone
Your library, chord files, and the audio you import or record are stored on your device. You can use the app
without an account.

## What the server receives
- **A random device ID and a guest token**, created on first launch, so the server can link your requests to
  your device and apply daily limits. It is not tied to your name, phone number or advertising ID.
- **Audio you choose to transcribe** (a file you import or a recording you make). It is uploaded so the server
  can analyze it, and kept so you can re-download it and so identical audio isn't analyzed twice.
- **Links you choose to transcribe**: a YouTube video ID or a SoundCloud URL. The server downloads that audio
  only to analyze it.
- **Analysis results** (chords, beats, key, tempo). These are shared between users who transcribe the same
  song, without any information about who requested it.
- **If you create an account**: your email address, a hashed password, and your library list (titles, sources,
  favorites) so it can sync between your devices.
- **If you buy Pro**: your purchase status, through Apple, Google and RevenueCat. We never see your payment
  details.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising.

## Microphone
The microphone is used only while you record a song to transcribe. The recording is uploaded only when you
choose to transcribe it.

## Services we use
- **DigitalOcean** hosts the server and stores uploaded audio and analysis files.
- **YouTube**: the in-app YouTube browser loads youtube.com, where Google's privacy policy applies.
- **A proxy provider** may be used to download audio from YouTube links. It sees the video, not who asked.
- **RevenueCat, Apple and Google** handle purchases, if you make one.

All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS).

## Deleting your data
Deleting a song in the app removes it from your device (and from your synced library if you have an account).
To delete your account and everything the server holds about you, email
[jordan879upbeat@gmail.com](mailto:jordan879upbeat@gmail.com) from the address on your account; we do it within
30 days.

## Children
MusicDecoded is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
